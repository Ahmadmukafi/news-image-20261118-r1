# The World in One Day — 18 November 2026

A newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one
image. The program copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`; the image
itself is drawn by `draw.py` with PIL — real text, no downloaded images,
no image model — so every headline stays legible.

## The stories on the page

- **Lead — Trump lands in Manila.** The US president arrives in the
  Philippines for Quad talks as the ASEAN summit wraps, ahead of a
  historic meeting with China's Xi.
- **Ukraine — Crimea bridge hit again.** Kyiv's drones strike the bridge
  to Crimea for a second day in a 1,336-drone barrage, as Russia pounds
  bridges in Kyiv.
- **The Gulf — escalation.** Iran's drones hit a US radar site in
  Bahrain; Washington strikes an IRGC missile base on Qeshm, and a
  tanker is attacked in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Also today.** Canada and China extend trade deals before APEC;
  Britain sanctions Israeli settlement firms; Cyclone Akvan nears the
  Bay of Bengal; MS Dhoni retires from the IPL and becomes CSK mentor.
